# Privacy Policy – Playlist Buttons for YouTube

**Effective date:** November 22, 2026

Playlist Buttons for YouTube ("the extension") is a Chrome extension that adds buttons to YouTube video thumbnails so you can add videos to your own YouTube playlists in one click. This policy explains what data the extension uses and why.

## Summary

- The extension has **no servers** of its own. Nothing you do is sent to the developer.
- It uses your Google sign-in **only** to call the official YouTube Data API on your behalf, and only when you click one of its buttons.
- It does **not** collect, sell, share or track your browsing activity. There are no ads and no analytics.

## Data the extension uses

1. **Google sign-in token (authentication information).** Lets the extension call the YouTube Data API for you, to check whether a video is already in a playlist and to add it. The token is obtained and kept by Chrome itself and is sent only to Google's YouTube Data API. The extension never stores it or sends it anywhere else.
2. **Video ID and playlist ID of the button you click.** Identify which video to add to which playlist. Sent only to the YouTube Data API, at the moment you click.
3. **Your button settings** (label, color and playlist ID for each button) **and the show/hide switch.** Stored in Chrome's own sync storage so they follow you across your Chrome browsers. Never sent to the developer.

The extension reads the YouTube pages you visit only to place its buttons on video thumbnails and to find a video's ID when you click a button. It does not record, store or send what you watch or browse.

## Google API Services and YouTube API Services

The extension's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to add videos to your playlists. It is not transferred to anyone else, not used for advertising, and not read by humans.

The extension uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's handling of your data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Sharing

The extension does not sell, rent or share any user data with third parties. The only party that receives data is Google, through the YouTube Data API, as needed to do what you ask.

## Retention and deletion

- The extension keeps no data on any server, so there is nothing for the developer to delete.
- Your button settings stay in Chrome until you remove them in the extension's popup or uninstall the extension.
- You can revoke the extension's access to your Google account at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

## Children

The extension is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
